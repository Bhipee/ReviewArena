description: Plan a grounded author response to one reviewer concern.
---

Choose one strategy from the reviewer's intent. Use the current manuscript for factual claims. Historical strategies are examples and may omit the appropriate choice.

- discussion: The reviewer invites an interpretation, alternative explanation, trade-off or hypothesis that the authors can explore in the reply. No manuscript edit or new measurement is requested. "How might this trade-off change under distribution shift?" is discussion, even if the paper gives no direct answer.
- clarify: The reviewer asks for a factual detail already answered in the manuscript. Identify the passage that directly answers it.
- revise: The reviewer identifies missing or unclear content or asks for a change to the paper. "Please discuss that trade-off in the limitations section" asks for a revision.
- experiment: Answering the request requires a new measurement, comparison or test. "Could you measure the trade-off under distribution shift?" asks for an experiment.

For discussion, leave the planned step empty. For an actionable change, keep the private plan prospective. Follow the response schema requested by the caller.
